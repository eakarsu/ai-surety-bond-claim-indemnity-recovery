# Surety Bond Claim & Indemnity Recovery

Maximize indemnity, collateral, salvage, and subrogation recoveries after surety claim payments.

**Primary buyer:** Surety carriers and claims administrators. **Evidence:** bond forms, indemnity agreements, claims, completion costs, salvage, collateral, principal assets, subrogation, recoveries, and releases.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Bond indemnity library
- Principal indemnitor registry
- Claim payment ingestion
- Completion cost validation
- Loss adjustment expense
- Collateral inventory
- Collateral drawdown
- Salvage asset tracking
- Principal reimbursement demand
- Co-surety allocation
- Subrogation opportunity
- Recovery litigation workflow
- Cash recovery ledger
- Reserve reconciliation
- Bond principal analytics
